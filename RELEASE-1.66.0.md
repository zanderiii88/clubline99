# CLUBLINE 1.66.0 — Streamlined and In-depth

Complete release including the earlier mobile usability changes, match controls and substitutions, all game assets and the five supplied Pixabay music tracks. No earlier ZIP is required.

## Management depth

New Career offers Streamlined and In-depth. Existing saves default to In-depth. Management depth can be changed in Options & Help between matches, retaining the season, players and results.

Streamlined has three primary destinations: Club, Team and Season. Club brings together the next fixture, pending decisions and a short expandable summary. Team retains XI and bench selection, tap/drag swaps and assistant selection, with formation and Attacking/Balanced/Defensive choices. Season retains fixtures, competition information, tables and the calendar; the top-bar date opens the calendar. Attention badges highlight pending decisions and urgent items.

Continue processes routine days and other clubs' fixtures until your next match, a meaningful decision, a selected calendar date or the end of the season. Pause and retained calendar targets remain available. Draws are processed automatically, with a short notice for your club's next tie, group or bye. Competition entries appear in the season briefing. The season length, competition structure and match rules are unchanged.

## Decisions and delegation

- Signing suggestions, sale offers, availability problems, contract decisions, youth prospects and affordable investment suggestions appear as short decision cards. Closed cards remain on Club. Optional decisions can be dismissed or deferred until after the next match; pending decisions survive saves and reloads.
- Transfers and upgrades use the existing confirmation reviews. Cancelling spends nothing. A sale suggestion only lists the player; it does not sell them automatically.
- The assistant handles training and routine renewals when the proposed wage increase is at most 10% and cash covers four weekly payrolls at the new wage total. The three highest-rated players and renewals outside those limits need a decision. Payroll still occurs on its normal dates.
- Unaccepted friendly invitations are declined in Streamlined and routine club event proposals are omitted. Already accepted fixtures/bookings remain. Transfers, youth promotions and facility purchases are never automatically approved.
- Pre-match preparation is one screen with optional XI/approach changes and Watch or Simulate. Watching proceeds directly to the live match. Live formation, approach and confirmed/cancelled substitutions remain available. Closing the report returns to Club without separate results/table walkthroughs.

In-depth retains the full management screens and existing calendar, preparation and post-match flows. Streamlined changes management responsibilities and presentation; it does not shorten the season or weaken opponents.

## Validation

Chromium mobile emulation at 390×844, 360×740 and 844×390 passed mode selection, reduced navigation, deferred/reloaded decisions, transfer and upgrade cancellation, calendar targets, preparation, live controls, direct report closure, mode switching and overflow checks. The In-depth mobile regression suite passed the existing selection, touch drag, calendar, tactics, report and substitution checks.

A full Streamlined season completed 54 managed matches and every scheduled fixture, including both domestic cups, ending on 5 June 1999. Monthly backups/reloads, routine renewal limits, explicit sale decisions and switching modes were checked. Domestic, Champions League and UEFA Cup application regressions passed. Asset checks verify all 68 stadiums, 408 kits, offline files, JavaScript syntax and unchanged roster data.

Physical Android responsiveness and layout still need user testing.

## Upload and try

Export a career backup first. Extract the ZIP and upload its contents to the root of the existing GitHub Pages game folder, replacing matching files. After publishing, Check for Updates / Load update and reopen. The title should show 1.66.0. Nothing has been deployed automatically.

For a combined phone test, choose Streamlined (or switch an existing career in Options & Help), Continue to a decision and defer it, prepare/watch a match, tap or drag a substitution and confirm/cancel it, close the report, use the calendar date/target, then switch back to In-depth. Check portrait and landscape layouts.
