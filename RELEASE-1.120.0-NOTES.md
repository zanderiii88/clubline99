# CLUBLINE 99 v1.120.0

## Stadium approaches and visual consistency
Scottish grounds now sit in grass around their current stadium footprint, rather than a large, permanently paved reserve. A connected perimeter walk and four approaches link the ground to the surrounding pavements. The paths adapt to the built sections, rear blocks and roof depths, and leave room for the future sections of active construction projects. They are decorative: no change to access ratings, attendance, bookings, capacity, finances or construction decisions.

The five shared Scottish district styles retain v1.119's road-safe building plots. Muted centre dashes now help define the four approach streets, stopping short of junctions. Scottish image/cache revisions request the new art on upgrade. These are schematic districts, not surveyed recreations of individual real neighbourhoods.

Low-stand camera framing now includes the floodlight masts. Bowl-only previews keep their tighter framing. This framing correction applies to other fixed grounds as well as Scotland. Active construction uses vertical scaffold panels, posts and crossbars instead of the previous sloping pitch-to-roof sheet; corner projects use segmented panels. Construction dates and closure rules are unchanged.
