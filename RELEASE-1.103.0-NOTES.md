# CLUBLINE 99 v1.103.0 - youth opportunities and follow-through

Youth and Player development now include a shared review of under-22 senior players and academy prospects. Listed positions link to current formation gaps, summer departures, contract risks and limited cover from the succession plan. A flagged gap is an opportunity to assess, not a promise the player can fill it.

Each player shows recorded competitive appearances and minutes for the managed club this season. Senior players also show tracked overall ability change and its tracking date. Academy exact ability stays hidden. Loan players are directed to loan playing-time reports because parent-club records exclude away appearances. Registration, recovery and suspension context remain advisory; no promotion, loan, selection or spending happens automatically.

Records can be incomplete in older careers. Minutes and ability change are separate observations; growth is not guaranteed or attributed to minutes. This is a read-only review with no new save fields.
