# Historical competition winners

Records stop at the end of 1997–98. Later records are generated by the career simulation, never copied from future real-world results. Existing club IDs respect career renaming. Additional historical-only clubs have fictional display names and do not create active squads.

Factual year/winner records checked on 13 October 2026:
- RSSSF, England champions: https://www.rsssf.org/tablese/engchamp.html
- RSSSF, second-tier champions: https://www.rsssf.org/tablese/eng2champ.html
- RSSSF, FA Challenge Cup finals: https://www.rsssf.org/tablese/engcuphist.html
- RSSSF, Football League Cup finals: https://www.rsssf.org/tablese/engleagcuphist.html
- RSSSF, European Champions' Cup/Champions League, Karel Stokkermans: https://www.rsssf.org/tablese/ec1.html
- RSSSF, UEFA Cup/Europa League: https://www.rsssf.org/tablese/ec3b.html
- Third-tier chronology and winners: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_winners_of_the_EFL_League_One_and_predecessors (references the Football League's past-winners archive and Playfair annuals).

Only factual winners and years are reproduced; descriptions are original. Thanks to RSSSF's historical researchers. Regional third-tier North and South champions are separately labelled. Wartime unofficial leagues and abandoned competitions are excluded. League Cup two-leg finals from 1961–66 use aggregate winners, not first-leg winners. European Cup titles are included in Champions League history; UEFA Cup history starts in 1971–72 and does not merge the Fairs Cup.
