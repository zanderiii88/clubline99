# CLUBLINE 99 — 1.70.0

Complete cumulative release, including 1.68.0 and 1.69.0. Extract the whole ZIP before uploading; keep its folder structure intact. Nothing has been deployed automatically.

## This release
- Loan agreements with interested clubs, three/six-month or season-end duration, wage contributions and optional recall after 28 days. The club retains at least 18 available players.
- Loan reports use recorded match minutes and ratings. Young players can develop through playing time; selection is not guaranteed. Returns exceeding the active squad limit wait for registration while remaining on payroll.
- Youth reports show a broad current-ability range alongside potential; investment in youth development narrows uncertainty.
- A maximum of 30 registered active players, with academy players separate.
- Seasonal world development, varied age-related retirement and new senior prospects to maintain AI squad depth and goalkeeper cover.
- Limited monthly AI transfers with ownership, contracts and club budgets, providing actual transfer news.
- Compressed large device saves, ordinary JSON backup exports, and season-transition save fixes.
- Domestic and European knockout draws can reveal one team at a time. Relevant late-season nearby-team results can become important news.

## Included from earlier builds
- 1.68.0: refreshed branding, expected-XI shirts, a measured-speed news ticker, and optional draw stops in Streamlined mode.
- 1.69.0: player attribute bars, comparisons, Players and Transfers search/filtering, stronger fictional free agents, contract-expiry filters and agreed summer arrivals.

## Scope and limitations
- Real-name search aliases cover the available mapped reference players; coverage is not complete.
- Summer-arrival agreements are paid club transfers, not a complete historical Bosman/pre-contract rules simulation.
- New free-agent pools and world prospects are fictional. Existing historical squad records remain unchanged.
- Loan destinations and playing time depend on the simulated schedule and team selection. This is a simplified loan market.
- Nationality flags, other-manager profiles, manager moves/sackings and associated news are queued for a following release and are not included here.
