## Before a public release
Prepare a practical checklist with current authoritative guidance, especially the treatment of player and manager names, club and competition names, branding, crests, kit designs, likenesses and historical databases. Review artwork, music, sound effects, fonts and code/dependency licences and record their origins and permitted uses. Decide the intended distribution and commercial model before assessing its requirements. Do not assume that a real-name toggle, fictional names or a free download alone settle permissions questions. Identify which decisions require professional advice.
