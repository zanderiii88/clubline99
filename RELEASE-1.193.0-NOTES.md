# CLUBLINE 99 v1.193.0

Vacancy papers now advertise clubs with a caretaker in charge. A board can open a search if there are no eligible papers after a dismissal. Jobs remain in the same country's pyramid and at the same level or lower.

Apply to up to two clubs at a time. Boards reply after three game days and can reject applications when the manager's record falls short. Advance to the next reply, or advance a week while waiting for another vacancy. Both vacancies and replies stay on the same scrollable desk, and pending applications survive a save and reload.

Install the cumulative update over the live v1.175 checkpoint or a later build through v1.192. Keep the existing assets and data.
