# CLUBLINE 99 v1.131.0

Adds independent Top-flight contender, Mid-level side and Lower-division side settings for both fictional lab XIs. Open Developer · Match test, then Team strength. Quick buttons select a mismatch or even talent tiers. Either selector restarts paused; tactical drafts remain available. Replay preserves strength, plan, profile and instructions; export includes both tiers and the resulting attributes.

Talent adjusts individual passing, finishing, tackling, technical, mental and physical attributes while retaining variation and squad style. No club-name result bonus or forced winner is applied. Goalkeepers now use handling for gathering chances and reflexes for reach. Player inspection includes these keeper attributes.

## Evidence and limits
180 seeded ten-minute passages (60 in each direction plus 60 equal-tier controls), alternating kickoff side. Mismatches: stronger side won 112, drew 6, lost 2; shots 1725–382 and goals 391–45. These extreme comparisons show direction, not realistic 90-minute scorelines. Scoring remains excessive. Equal-tier controls: Blue won 29, Red 12, drew 19, exposing a side/setup imbalance to investigate. Teams at the same tier retain different individual attributes; mirrored identical-squad experiments remain outstanding. Completion percentage did not consistently favour the stronger side; pass choice/reception needs calibration. No claim of historically realistic results or validated upset frequencies.

Deterministic replays, tier round trips without stacking, bounded positions, 30 spatial passages, 48 tactical passages, 48 non-stalling passages, saved loose-ball recovery, dribbling decisions, navigation in both career modes, source and cache checks passed. Mobile browser checked at 320px: strength selection, reversed teams, paused restart, suitability and playback. Physical phone performance still requires testing.

Normal career simulation, saves, data and assets are unchanged. This remains a simplified ground-ball developer lab with no offside, cards, aerial play, complete set pieces or substitutions. Prepared build only; not published or a release candidate.
