# CLUBLINE 99 v1.173.0

- Opening stories vary with the club’s squad and fixtures. A young player can earn praise in training, or supporters can prepare the ground when the first home league match is close.
- Later club news can react to a well-attended home win or a heavy home league defeat. Transport and press stories use upcoming away fixtures and current club morale.
- The opening event shows the actual changes to players, club morale and supporter goodwill. Subsequent stories and their effects can be reviewed in Club life & stadium events.
- Streamlined careers receive automatic stories without added decision stops. In-depth careers continue to get occasional choices. Existing stories and acknowledgements survive an update.
