# CLUBLINE 99 v1.126.0 - quieter advancement and visual building

## Stadium controls
Select individual sections or toggle a whole side, then use the left/right arrows for Stand, Roof and Material/colour. The highlighted preview and quote update together. Friendly tier/cover labels replace technical stand names in the main controls. Material/colour cycles through the three existing finish presets; this is not an arbitrary colour picker. Specifications, saved plans and phasing remain folded away. Previewing never spends money; Review construction and Confirm & pay remain separate.

New structural quotes allow substantially longer works: one double-tier section starts at 180 days and a third-tier section at 240 days, with additional time for larger projects. Single-tier structural works start at 98 days; simple standing/bleacher work, roofs and finishes are shorter. These are gameplay estimates, not contractor estimates. Already paid projects keep their agreed opening dates. Closures and affected fixtures use the revised quotes.

## Calendar advancement
Simulation choices appear only after Advance To This Date or the next-match action. Cancel closes the choices without moving time. During a run, a dedicated progress screen replaces intermediate matches and event screens, with no live-match audio. The recap lists results, the current league position and points; key events are available in a collapsed section.

Stop ends the run and clears its destination. Completed time and results are retained. Ordinary Continue cannot restart the stopped journey. A decision-triggered stop can still retain its destination for explicit continuation. Old saved journeys labelled Paused by you are cleared when loaded. Delegation policies and safeguards from v1.125 remain, including the season boundary and inability to select a legal lineup.

## Softer atmosphere
Occasional details now fade in and out, with especially low bird, phone and whistle levels. Leaving a scene fades its detail out over half a second while the continuous background floor remains. Muting, hiding or pausing still stops playback immediately. Recorded files and credits are unchanged. This is mix/envelope refinement; the final judgement still needs listening on the user's speakers and phone.

Audio direction remains a default intro theme followed by quieter ambient, atmospheric main-game sound/music. No new music is included in this build. The larger moving-shirt/ball match view and score-balance tuning remain separate roadmap work.

## Compatibility and verification
No fresh career is required. v1.125 is the uploaded checkpoint used for this update. Existing paid stadium projects, databases, recorded audio and stadium-scene assets are preserved. Export a backup before installing. Nothing is published automatically.

Automated coverage includes both management modes: section cycling and mixed selection; unchanged preview balance; 180-day double-tier quoting; one-time construction payment/completion; requested-only simulation controls; actual delegated results and league recaps; Stop during an asynchronous run; compressed reload/backup; and no resumption through ordinary Continue. Existing two-month simulation journeys, project progress, itemised construction safeguards, contextual audio, event-linked match cues and exact offline module URLs are checked. Soft-audio checks use virtual playback timing; they do not replace physical listening. See review/*.json for bounded results.

Desktop browser: a fresh Streamlined Middlesbrough career verified section/material cycling, unchanged preview cash, fixed-site rejection, requested-only simulation choices, the progress screen, three August results and league recap, then a stopped longer run with twelve completed results. Ordinary Continue opened a squad-availability decision at the stopped date instead of resuming December advancement. The In-depth browser journey was not repeated; it is covered by the separate handler tests.
