# CLUBLINE 99 v1.91.0

## Club identity
Unnecessary nickname suffixes have been removed from a range of English and Scottish names. Aberdon Granite is now Aberdon; Motherwell Steel is Motherwell. The two Dundee clubs use Dundon and Dundon United. United, City, Athletic, Rovers and similar club-name distinctions are retained where useful. Club IDs, kits, grounds, rivalries and competition membership are unchanged.

## Training and youth
Training provision and youth quality are now rated independently, with half-star steps. English Premier clubs generally have stronger starting training provision, without receiving modern facilities that opened after 1998. Examples: Mancaster United 4.0 training / 4.5 youth; Armoury 3.0 / 4.0; Glasgow Clover 2.5 / 3.5; Glasgow Royal 2.5 / 3.0; Aberdon 1.5 / 3.0; Dundon 1.5 / 2.5; Dundon United 2.0 / 3.5.

All star ratings and generic club descriptions are editorial game assessments, not historical facility audits. Some club histories establish useful dates, but do not justify exact star scores or claims that an individual club trained in a particular public park in 1998. The game does not present those guesses as verified history.

## Investment and development
Projects add half a star and take 42, 84 or 126 days according to target level. Smaller clubs have cheaper entry steps. Quotes show the one-off price, completion date and additional weekly upkeep before confirmation. Upkeep starts on completion, is charged with Monday running costs, survives save/backup/reload and continues into later seasons. Only one project may run at a time; the existing cooldown remains. There is no automatic spending by the assistant.

The assistant explains whether to protect cash, strengthen available squad cover or consider training/youth investment. Streamlined users can review the same projects through Club budget → Training & youth investment. In-depth users have the finance and facilities routes.

Positional training now uses the upgraded training rating rather than only the opening rating. Overall development explanations use the actual engine inputs: age, potential ceiling, injury/registration status, training provision, focus and recent match form. Youth upgrades retain the existing intake/development rules and do not reroll existing potential.

## Responsiveness and match controls
League tables are reused only within a single render, avoiding repeated calculations without retaining stale standings across gameplay changes. Comparisons initially render 80 options; search/filter and Show more retain access to all candidates. A live match opening on line-ups no longer constructs the stadium SVG until Stadium is selected. Pause, speed, view switching and substitution controls remain shared between modes. A paused injury/red-card incident stays in the latest-action feed. Landscape substitution drawers no longer inherit an off-screen fifth grid row.

## Validation and use
Start a fresh career for the new opening ratings and names. Existing career editions are not migrated. Development/investment checks cover all 124 English/Scottish data clubs, including the 88 playable clubs. Browser emulation covers both management modes at narrow portrait and landscape sizes, investment confirmation, pending/completed save and backup, invalid upkeep rejection, comparison paging/search, live view switching and substitutes controls. These checks do not constitute testing on a physical phone or a complete long-running career.
