# Historical starting managers

v1.125 adds verified starting-name mappings for the 20 English Premier League clubs and all 20 clubs in the two playable Scottish divisions. These use the existing real-names option alongside players, clubs and grounds. The user's own manager name is preserved. Other clubs and newly generated coaches remain fictional until researched.

Use the opening appointments, not the end-of-season personnel tables. Examples include Alex Miller at Aberdeen, Tommy McLean at Dundee United, Bert Paton at Dunfermline, Harri Kampman at Motherwell, Paul Sturrock at St Johnstone, Kenny Dalglish at Newcastle, Christian Gross at Tottenham, George Graham at Leeds and Roy Hodgson at Blackburn. Liverpool's opening Roy Evans/Gérard Houllier partnership is represented by one combined manager entry because the current job model has one manager per club.

Names and starting nationality are historical; tactical ratings, career behaviour and biographies are game estimates. Estimated ages are not displayed for historical starting managers. Subsequent changes follow game performance rather than forcing historical sackings. Names attach to the original person ID and follow that person if they move. Switching names preserves jobs, results, relationships and recorded events; known manager names in saved narrative text are translated at display time.
