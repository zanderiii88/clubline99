# CLUBLINE 99 v1.90 finance and Dundee review

All money is in pounds at 1998–99 scale. Figures in the game are editorial estimates for playability, not audited balances or exact individual salary records. Opening cash is available club cash; it is neither turnover nor a separate transfer-only fund. The transfer guide subtracts four weekly payrolls; the manager must also allow for operating costs. No historical biographies or individual salary claims have been invented.

Opening hierarchy: Mancaster United £24m; Glasgow Clover £8.5m; Glasgow Royal £8m; Aberdon Granite and Hearts of Midshire £2.6m each; Dundee Tangerine £1.6m; Hibernia £1.5m; Kilmarnoch £1.25m; Dundee Dark Blues £1.15m; Motherwell Steel £1.05m; Saint Johnston £850k; Dunferm Athletic £650k. Scottish First Division ranges from £160k to Hibernia's £1.5m. English clubs have individually differentiated cash, wages, commercial income and operating costs. Clover's small cash and commercial advantage over Royal is a requested game-balance choice, not a historical accounting claim.

Weekly wages, commercial income and club running costs settle on Mondays, including the summer. Match operating costs remain a separate event cost. Attendance varies around a club-specific baseline. Promotion/relegation scales commercial income relative to the club's original division. Values use ability, age, potential and salary level; contract length still affects fees. Renewals begin from the player's existing wage. Incoming demands also consider similarly rated players at the buyer. AI buying retains its squad payroll reserve and records the new personal wage.

Dundee retains researched player parodies, with corrected period shirt numbers and a 25-player season squad. Added missing Langfield, Garcin, Tweed and Boyack parodies. This is a season roster, not an exact opening-day registration list; it follows the game's existing season-roster convention. Mid-table team strength is an editorial estimate, not a scripted fifth-place finish. Denholm Park uses a period-reference capacity of 11,760. The ground and kits remain stylised period interpretations, rather than exact architectural reproductions.
